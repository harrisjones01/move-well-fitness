Privacy Policy

What data MoveWell collects, why, how long we keep it, and your rights. Covers cookies, analytics, Google advertising and health information you volunteer.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who we are

MoveWell is published by MoveWell Media AB, Södra Vägen 18, 412 54 Gothenburg, Sweden. For any privacy question, or to exercise a right described below, write to hello@movewell.fit. We are the data controller for personal data collected through this website.

This policy covers the website, our newsletter and the contact form. It does not cover third-party sites we link to.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Your email address, if you subscribe to the newsletter.
  • Your name, email, message, age group and experience level if you use the contact form — the last two only so a coach can answer usefully.
  • Anything you send us by email, including injury history you choose to describe.

Information collected automatically

  • Pages visited, referring page, approximate location (city level), device type, browser and screen size.
  • Aggregated statistics about which guides are read and for how long.
  • Cookie identifiers used for analytics and, where you consent, advertising.

Special category data

If you describe an injury or health condition in a message to us, that is health data under the GDPR. We process it only to answer your question, on the basis of your explicit consent (given by ticking the consent box), keep it for 24 months, and never share it. Please share only what is necessary for us to help.

Why we use it (legal bases)

  • Legitimate interests — to run, secure and improve the website; to understand which guides are useful.
  • Consent — for analytics and advertising cookies, and for processing any health details you volunteer.
  • Contract — to deliver the newsletter you asked for.
  • Legal obligation — to keep records where tax or other law requires it.

Advertising and Google

This site is supported by display advertising. We use Google AdSense and may use other advertising partners. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • Where required by law we ask for consent before any advertising cookie is set, using a Google-certified consent management platform.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Advertisers never receive your name, email address, message content or any health information.

In health-adjacent content we apply additional restrictions on what advertising we accept — see our <a href="advertise.html">advertising page</a>.

How long we keep data

  • Newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe, plus 30 days in backup.
  • Contact messages (including health details you volunteer): 24 months.
  • Analytics data: 14 months, then aggregated permanently.
  • Server logs: 30 days.

Who we share data with

A hosting/CDN provider, an email newsletter platform, an analytics provider and our advertising partners, each under a data processing agreement. We do not sell personal data and we do not share your messages with advertisers.

Where data is transferred outside the EEA we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. Write to us and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to your national data protection authority. In Sweden that is Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY).

Children

Our children's activity content is written for parents and carers, not for children to use alone. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

Material changes update the date at the top of this page, and significant changes affecting newsletter subscribers are announced by email.

Questions about this policy? Email hello@movewell.fit and we will reply within five working days.
