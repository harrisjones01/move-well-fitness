Exercise is at your own risk

This is the most important section on this page. The content on MoveWell is general information about exercise, not medical advice, and it cannot account for your individual health, injuries or circumstances. By using the site you accept that you exercise at your own risk.

  • Get medical clearance before starting if you have a heart or lung condition, uncontrolled blood pressure, diabetes, are pregnant or postpartum, have had recent surgery, or have not exercised in years.
  • Stop immediately and seek medical advice if you experience chest pain, dizziness, faintness, unusual shortness of breath or joint pain.
  • Never train through sharp pain. Muscle effort is expected; joint pain is not.
  • Follow the safety notes on each guide. They are there because they matter, not as decoration.
