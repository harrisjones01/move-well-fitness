Advertise with MoveWell

A high-intent fitness audience that reads technique guides to the end. Advertising is clearly labelled, never placed inside instructions, and never influences a recommendation.

The audience

31,000Weekly readers
165kMonthly pageviews
4:20Avg. time on guide
61%Mobile traffic

Readers arrive from organic search mid-way through a training decision — how to squat, what to do about a stiff hip, whether HIIT is right for them. That intent produces strong engagement for equipment, apparel, gyms and recovery products.

Available placements

  • Leaderboard (728×90 / responsive) — below the header. Reserved height, no layout shift.
  • In-content rectangle (336×280) — after the step-by-step instructions, never inside them.
  • Sticky sidebar (300×600) — desktop only, on guides and articles.
  • Newsletter sponsorship — one primary slot in the Sunday send to ~14,000 subscribers.
  • Sponsored guide — clearly labelled, coach-written, never a paid rating.

What we will not run

No weight-loss pills, appetite suppressants, "detox" products, unproven recovery gadgets, gambling, tobacco or crypto speculation. No interstitials, pop-unders, autoplay audio or anything that covers the instructions on mobile. Health-adjacent advertising is held to a higher standard here than the network minimum.

Technical setup

Static build served over HTTPS, Google AdSense / Ad Manager tags with a certified CMP for GDPR and TCF v2.2, reserved heights on every slot to keep Cumulative Layout Shift near zero, and ads.txt published at the root.

Get the media kit

Email partners@movewell.fit with your brand and objective and we will send current traffic breakdown, rates and availability. Typical response: one working day.

Editorial firewall

Commercial partners never see a guide before publication and have no approval over ratings or recommendations. Sponsored content is labelled at the top of the page and never carries a paid rating. See our editorial policy.
